# Polytopia self-play bot: replays and results

A neural network that plays The Battle of Polytopia in the World Championship format (1v1, Might,
Lakes, small map), first trained on human tournament games and then improved by self-play, one
generation at a time. This site shows each generation's results (vs the game's hardest bot,
Crazy; vs the previous generation; vs generation 0) and replays of its games, updated
automatically after every generation.

Open the site: https://bengreff.github.io/polytopia-ai/

Source code, training pipeline and research log: [github.com/bengreff/polytopia](https://github.com/bengreff/polytopia)

An unofficial research project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Midjiwan AB. The Battle of
Polytopia is a trademark of Midjiwan AB. No game code or game files are published here: the
replays are recorded board states, drawn by the site's own simple renderer.
